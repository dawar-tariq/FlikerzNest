Watchlist basics

How to Build a Watchlist You Will Actually Use

A watchlist is useful only when it helps you make a decision. The goal is not to save every interesting title; it is to keep a short, organised queue that makes your next choice easier.

1. Save with a reason

Before adding a film or series, decide why it belongs on your list. You might be saving it for a quiet evening, for a particular genre or because someone recommended it. A reason makes it easier to remember why you saved it.

2. Separate the next few titles from the rest

Keep a small group of titles that you are genuinely likely to start soon. Leave everything else in a longer-term queue. When the short list is empty, promote a few titles from the longer list instead of endlessly scrolling.

3. Update the status honestly

Move a title out of the “plan to watch” state once you start it. Mark a series as completed only when you have finished it. Accurate statuses make your list easier to scan and help prevent duplicate saves.

4. Review the list regularly

Remove titles that no longer interest you. A watchlist is not a collection you need to preserve forever. A short review every few weeks keeps it relevant.

5. Keep your list connected to your account
